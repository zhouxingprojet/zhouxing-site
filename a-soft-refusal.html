A Soft
Refusal

A space
before response.

A Soft Refusal is a generative work about algorithmic attention and cognitive sovereignty. It begins with blur: the residue of an image copied and compressed, but also a zone that temporarily resists identification, classification, and prediction.

The work moves through six emotional states: fragile clarity, attraction and capture, algorithmic repetition, fragmentation, friction, and the slow recovery of agency.

Moving the mouse or a finger disturbs the image and feeds its impulse to reproduce. Remaining still forces the system to encounter a pause it cannot immediately convert into data.

This stillness is not withdrawal. It becomes a small but real gesture: recovering enough time to choose the next movement.
